Life

The early years of Roger Federer one of tennis's greatest players

Family

Roger Federer was born on August 8, 1981, in Basel, Switzerland. His father, Robert Federer, is Swiss, and his mother, Lynette Federer, is originally from South Africa.

Childhood Interests

Before focusing on tennis, Federer enjoyed a lots of sports. Like soccer, basketball, badminton, and table tennis. Soccer was one of his favorite sports.

School Years

Federer attended school in Basel and was known for being competitive. While he enjoyed sports, he also worked to balance his education with his growing interest in athletics.

Discovering Tennis

Roger began playing tennis at a young age and showed a natural talent. By the age of 11, he was one of the top junior players in Switzerland. His success at youth tournaments convinced him to pursue tennis more seriously.
