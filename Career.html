Roger Federer

About

Roger Federer is a former Swiss tennis player. He is considered one of the greatest tennis players in history.

Career Highlights

  • Turned professional in 1998
  • Won 20 Grand Slam singles titles
  • Won 8 Wimbledon titles
  • Held the World No. 1 ranking for 310 weeks
  • Retired in 2022

Legacy

style Roger Federer's legacy is one of greatness. He is widely regarded as one of the best tennis players ever, known for his incredible achievements, sportsmanship, and positive impact on the game of tennis.
