Roger Federer's Tennis Racket

The equipment used by one of the greatest tennis players ever.

Wilson Pro Staff RF97 Autograph

Roger Federer played most of his later career with the Wilson Pro Staff RF97 Autograph. This racket was designed with Federer's input and became one of the most popular rackets among advanced players.

Key Specifications

Brand: Wilson

Model: Pro Staff RF97 Autograph

Head Size: 97 square inches

Weight: Approximately 340 grams (unstrung)

String Pattern: 16 x 19

Designed With: Roger Federer

Why Federer Chose This Racket

Roger Federer chose his racket to be like this because it allowed him to have modern heavy power why of playing tennis and also help him play on all kinds of playing surfaces

Legacy

The Wilson Pro Staff RF97 Autograph became iconic because of its association with Roger Federer. Many tennis fans and players purchased the racket to experience equipment inspired by one of the sport's greatest champions.
